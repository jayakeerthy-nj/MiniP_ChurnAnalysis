ML Pipeline

End-to-End Processing Stages

Stage 01 — Ingestion
📡

Data Generation & Ingestion

Synthetic banking data generator produces 12,000 customer profiles with realistic Kaggle-inspired distributions — balances, tenure, products, digital usage, and complaints.

PythonNumPyJSONCSV
Stage 02 — Feature Engineering
⚙️

Feature Matrix Construction

14 engineered features including engagement scores, balance velocity, credit utilization, digital adoption index, and complaint resolution rates feed the model pipeline.

PandasStandardScaler14 Features
Stage 03 — Classification
🛡️

XGBoost Churn Classifier

Gradient-boosted tree ensemble trained on 10,000 samples with stratified split. Isotonic calibration transforms raw scores into true probability estimates.

XGBoostRandomForestLogisticRegression
Stage 04 — Segmentation
📊

K-Means Behavioral Clustering

Unsupervised clustering groups customers into 5 behavioral segments — Dormant, High-Value, Active Digital, Friction-Prone, and New Growth profiles.

K-Meansk=5Silhouette Score
Stage 05 — Explainability
🔍

SHAP Feature Attribution

TreeExplainer decomposes every prediction into auditable per-feature contributions, enabling transparent risk driver analysis for compliance officers.

SHAPTreeExplainerForce Plots
Stage 06 — Synthesis
✨

Gemini AI Summaries

Generative AI narratives synthesize customer 360° dossiers into executive retention briefs with actionable recommendations and fee waiver strategies.

Gemini APIRule-based Fallback
